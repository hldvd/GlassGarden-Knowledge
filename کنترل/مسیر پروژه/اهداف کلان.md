## خلاصه
This file provides a concise operational summary of the project's overarching goals (derived from the full reference document [[اهداف کلان پروژه]]). The six overarching goals guide the selection of the current project plan «طرح فعلی» (بخش «انتخاب طرح فعلی»).

## więź
The overarching goals are:
1. Creating an accessible nature form in limited space
2. Enhancing the quality of natural and peaceful experience  
3. Establishing control and sustainability of a small ecosystem
4. Reducing dependence on continuous human maintenance
5. Creating an expandable architecture for different product variants
6. Translating interdisciplinary knowledge into real product realization

**Note:** Detailed explanations, reasoning, and linkage to prior path are available in [[اهداف کلان پروژه]].
